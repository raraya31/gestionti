Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for small businesses in the Laurentides and on the North Shore.

What is Gestion TI Informatique?

Gestion TI Informatique is a managed IT services provider (MSP) based in Mirabel, Quebec. We look after IT for small and mid-sized businesses: user support, cybersecurity, Microsoft 365, backup, networking and automation. We are a Microsoft Partner.

Which areas do you serve?

Mirabel, Saint-Eustache, Sainte-Thérèse and Blainville, plus the Laurentides and the North Shore (Rive-Nord). On site when needed, remote the rest of the time.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Mainly three: car dealerships, warehouses and logistics, and professional offices. If you're in another field, call anyway and we'll tell you honestly whether we're a good fit.

Who answers when I call?

Me or my team. Not a call centre.

How much do managed services cost?

It's by quote (sur soumission). Three monthly plans: Bronze, Silver (Argent) and Gold (Or), based on the number of users and servers. We start with a free assessment, then send the price in writing.

Can you move our email to Microsoft 365?

Yes. As a Microsoft Partner we handle the full migration (email, files, Teams, SharePoint), secure configuration and licence management, with minimal downtime.

Do you help with cybersecurity and Quebec's Law 25?

Yes. We set up the basics that matter for a small business (MFA, endpoint and email protection, tested backups) and help you understand your Law 25 obligations.

Do you supply hardware?

Yes. Laptops, workstations, servers and peripherals, delivered configured and ready to use.

What language is service offered in?

Our service is offered in French. This page is in English so you can find us; feel free to reach out.

How do I reach you?

Phone (514) 742-6455, email info@gestionti.info. I'm based in Mirabel, QC. Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m. (Eastern).

Do you build websites?

No. That's not our work. We look after what sits around a site: email, the domain name and security.

Do you repair computers for individuals?

No. We work with businesses only, mostly small and mid-sized companies on the North Shore and in the Laurentides.
